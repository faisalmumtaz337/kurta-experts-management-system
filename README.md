# 🧵 Kurta Experts Management System

A comprehensive Laravel-based garment business management system designed to streamline customer records, measurements, tailoring orders, employee workflows, payroll management, invoicing, and payment tracking.

---

## 📌 Overview

Kurta Experts Management System is a business solution built for tailoring and garment operations. It helps manage the complete workflow from customer registration and measurements to order processing, employee work tracking, wage management, billing, and payment history.

The project focuses on improving operational efficiency through an organized and user-friendly management system.

---

## ✨ Key Features

### 👤 Customer Management

* Add, edit, update, and manage customer records
* Store customer measurements
* Maintain complete customer profiles

### 📏 Measurement Management

* Save detailed body measurements
* Update measurements for future orders
* Generate measurement slips

### 📦 Order Management

* Create and manage tailoring orders
* Track order status
* Associate orders with customers and measurements

### 💰 Payment Management

* Record customer payments
* Track outstanding balances
* Maintain payment history and ledger

### 👨‍🔧 Employee Management

* Manage employee profiles
* Record employee work history
* Track assigned tasks

### 💵 Payroll & Wages

* Calculate employee wages
* Maintain payment history
* Generate payroll records

### 🧾 Invoice Generation

* Thermal receipt support
* Printable order invoices
* Measurement slips

### 🔐 Authentication & Security

* Login authentication
* Role-based middleware protection
* Admin access control

### 🔎 Search System

* Reusable search implementation using Laravel Traits
* Efficient filtering across multiple modules
